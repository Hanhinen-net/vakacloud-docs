We will be performing important maintenance and service work on the VakaCloud platform this coming Saturday, October 31. Through regular maintenance, we ensure that the system's performance and data security remain at the highest possible level.
<!-- truncate -->

### Impacts of the Maintenance

The maintenance work will be carried out on Saturday, October 31, between 09:00 and 15:00. Although the update is performed outside of general weekday working hours, we ask units providing weekend care to take the timing of this break into account in advance.

There will be service interruptions during the maintenance window, during which the use of and logging into the application will be temporarily unavailable. If you are logged in when the break begins, please log in again once it has ended.
