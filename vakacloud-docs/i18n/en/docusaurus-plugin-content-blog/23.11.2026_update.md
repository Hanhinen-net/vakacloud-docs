The VakaCloud Support Portal (portal.vakacloud.fi) now includes electronic signatures and a few other improvements that make it easier to get started with and manage the service. Below is a summary of what has changed, who it is for and where to find it.
<!-- truncate -->

### Electronic Signatures

You can now send documents for electronic signing from the Support Portal. The signing service can be either **SignHero** or **Netvisor Sign**. An organisation uses one service at a time.

**Who it is for:** daycare management and staff who send contracts and other documents for signing.

**How it works:**

* A user with the Signer right opens **Signatures → Start signing**, adds the PDF files and chooses the signers: only themselves, themselves and others, or only others. There can be up to 20 signers, and they can also be imported from a CSV file.
* Signers only need a name and an email address. They do not need a VakaCloud account.
* The signing method is either email signature or strong authentication (online banking credentials or mobile certificate).
* Signers receive an automatic reminder every three days, and a request expires after 14 days.
* The sender or the organisation admin can cancel a pending request. Completed and cancelled requests are listed on the **Signed** and **Cancelled** tabs.

**Differences between the services:**

* **SignHero:** the signing method is chosen for the whole document (email signature by default), and signers can sign in parallel. A document can include up to 20 PDF files, each up to 10 MB. Each sender connects their own SignHero account under **Settings → Profile → SignHero account**.
* **Netvisor Sign:** the signing method is chosen per signer, and signers are invited in order. Strong authentication is the default when it is available in the signer's country (Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and the Netherlands); otherwise email signature is used. A document can include up to 10 PDF files, up to 15 MB in total. The organisation uses its own Netvisor Sign account, and the admin can choose which Netvisor Sign folder each document type is saved to.

**Getting started:** the organisation admin requests the service under **Organisation → Integrations**, accepts the price and terms, and grants Signer rights under **Organisation and users**. The service cannot be switched while requests are pending.

**Pricing:**

* **SignHero:** €12 / month per sender with the Signer right (excl. VAT). Email signatures are included; strong signatures cost €1.50 each. Billed on the VakaCloud invoice.
* **Netvisor Sign:** billed directly by Netvisor. The integration requires at least the Professional package. Current prices: [Netvisor Sign pricing](https://netvisorsign.fi/hinnoittelu/).

### Integrations Page

On the **Organisation → Integrations** page, the organisation admin can see the available modules and their status (for example Active, Awaiting approval or Rejected), along with each module's description, prices and what data is transferred. The admin can request a module to be turned on or off. A module is activated once VakaCloud has approved the request. More about integrations: [Integrations](https://vakacloud.fi/integraatiot/).

### Billing and Subscriptions

Under **Organisation → Billing**, the admin can see the organisation's VakaCloud subscription and choose to pay either by card or by e-invoice. The e-invoice address is checked against the public e-invoice address register before the request is sent for confirmation. VakaCloud support confirms the number of children that is billed.

Existing customers do not need to do anything because of this change. Billing continues as before, and you only need to use the page if you want to review your subscription details or change your payment method.

### Price Estimate for New Customers

New customers can submit a sign-up request at portal.vakacloud.fi/en/register. The form shows a price estimate based on the number of children; for Finnish customers, VAT of 25.5% is added to the price.

### Video for Staff

A video has been added to the Support Portal Knowledge Base showing the everyday tasks in the staff panel: child details, groups, events and messages. When logged in, you can find it under **Knowledge Base → Video for staff**. It is available to staff and admins.

### Guides

Detailed guides are available in the VakaCloud Support Portal Knowledge Base, for example *Signatures*, *Sending a document for signing*, *Setting up SignHero*, *Setting up Netvisor Sign*, *Integrations* and *Company details and billing*. If you have any questions, please contact us through support requests in the Support Portal.
