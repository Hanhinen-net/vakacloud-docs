Financial administration in early childhood education has traditionally required manual data transfer between spreadsheets, emails, and accounting systems. We have launched the new Guardian Invoicing in VakaCloud, seamlessly combining the daycare's daily operations and financial management into one place.
<!-- truncate -->

### Everything in one ecosystem

With the new feature, invoices are generated automatically directly from real everyday data, such as care contracts and realized care hours. We are not building a new, disconnected sales ledger for you; instead, we help you transfer the ready data to where your finances are already managed.

### What does this mean in practice?

* **Automation and time savings:** The system automatically generates period invoices based on templates created just once.
* **One view for guardians:** Guardians can view all their invoices directly in the VakaCloud portal alongside their other daycare information.
* **Flexible export options:** You can collect payments via the integrated payment channel or export invoice data directly to your accounting system.

### Start using the feature today

Reduce manual work and enable Guardian Invoicing today. Detailed quick guides for activating the feature, creating invoice templates, and configuring accounting settings can be found in the **VakaCloud Community** support portal.
