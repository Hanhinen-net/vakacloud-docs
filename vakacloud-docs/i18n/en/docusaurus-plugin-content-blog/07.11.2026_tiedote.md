We will be performing important maintenance and service work on the VakaCloud platform this coming Saturday, November 7.
<!-- truncate -->

### Updates Included in the Maintenance

* **Security Updates:** Continuous infrastructure improvements to maintain our data security standards.
* **Payroll Export for Shifts:** Enhancements to the HR settings and payroll export flow to streamline administrative work.
* **Message Timestamps:** Added clear dates to the Communication Center to improve the tracking of dialogue between the daycare and home.
* **General Improvements:** Minor system corrections and bug fixes to optimize daily usability.

### Impacts of the Maintenance

The maintenance work will be carried out on Saturday, November 7, between 09:00 and 15:00. Although the update is performed outside of general weekday working hours, we ask units providing weekend care to take the timing of this break into account in advance.

There will be service interruptions during the maintenance window, during which the use of and logging into the application will be temporarily unavailable. If you are logged in when the break begins, please log in again once it has ended.
