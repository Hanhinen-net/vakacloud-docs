We will be performing an important security and maintenance update on the VakaCloud service today, Tuesday, September 29th, starting at 19:00. With this update, we are strengthening the platform's login security as part of our continuous development efforts.

### New Features and Improvements

* **Proactive Security Update:** We have made proactive improvements to the backend authentication mechanisms and password management. This measure ensures that the service's data security and the protection of user data remain at the highest possible level.
* **System Improvements:** The update includes general backend optimizations that improve the reliability and overall smoothness of the service.

### Impacts of the Maintenance

The update will be performed starting at 19:00. There will be a short service interruption, and using or logging into the application will be temporarily suspended. Please log in again if your session expires during the update.
