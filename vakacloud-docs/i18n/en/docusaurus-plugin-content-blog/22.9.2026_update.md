We will be performing scheduled updates and maintenance on the VakaCloud service on Friday, September 25th, starting at 18:00. With this update, we are introducing highly requested improvements to the shift planner alongside minor system fixes.
<!-- truncate -->

### New Features and Improvements

* **Selective Shift Copying:** The shift planning tool has been enhanced. You can now freely select which individual shifts you want to copy from one employee to another. This eliminates manual data entry in situations where employees share some, but not all, shifts in their schedules.
* **Minor Fixes and System Improvements:** This update includes a set of minor bug fixes and backend optimizations that improve the overall smoothness and reliability of the service.

### Impacts of the Maintenance

The update will be performed starting at 18:00. There will be service interruptions, and logging into or using the application may be temporarily unavailable. Please log in again if your session expires during the update.
